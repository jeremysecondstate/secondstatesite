SECONDSTATE

Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how SecondState handles information connected with the Auction Watch SMS program. It applies to information submitted when a person asks to receive, receives, or responds to SecondState text messages.

Information we collect

When you participate in the SMS program, we may collect:

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • The date and method of your consent.
  • Your messaging preferences and auction-watch selections.
  • Records of messages sent to or received from your number.
  • Help, opt-out, and re-subscription requests.

How we use the information

We use SMS-program information to:

  • Send the auction-watch reminders you requested.
  • Maintain and document your messaging consent.
  • Process opt-out, help, and re-subscription requests.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the messaging service.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, carrier, and telecommunications-industry requirements.

Mobile information and consent

SecondState does not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

We may provide limited information to service providers that help us operate the program, including telecommunications, hosting, security, and technology providers. Those providers may use the information only to perform services for SecondState and not for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention and security

We retain consent and messaging records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the program, document compliance, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies, up to one message per day, and message and data rates may apply.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to hello@secondstate.art.

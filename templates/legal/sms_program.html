What the messages contain

Messages may include an artist name, artwork or lot title, lot number, auction house, auction date and time, estimate, status change, and a direct link to the public auction-lot page.

Program disclosures

  • Message frequency varies, up to one message per day.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
  • Reply STOP to cancel.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or email hello@secondstate.art.
  • Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Consent process

Individuals learn about the program outside of SMS through an existing business relationship, an in-person conversation, or an email directing them to this public page. The individual initiates consent from their own mobile phone by texting START. SecondState does not send a text message asking the individual to provide consent.

After the START request is received, SecondState records the request before adding the number to its internal opted-in recipient list. Messages are sent only to numbers that completed this process. A STOP request removes the number from future Auction Watch messages.
